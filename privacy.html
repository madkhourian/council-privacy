Council Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · Bundle ID app.council.table

This is what the current build actually does. Nothing here is a promise about a future server.

Who we are

Council is a deliberation table: one question, three seats, a Chair’s verdict. The operator of this app is Mohammad Saeed Madkhourian. There is no Council backend that receives your keys or chats.

Council is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google. Seat names are labels, not official apps.

What Council stores on your device

All of it stays in this browser or app’s localStorage on this device:

  • The archive — questions, seat answers, verdicts, and challenges.
  • API keys you paste for Live mode.
  • Demo vs Live, which seats are on, and whether you have seen the welcome.
  • Today’s free-sitting count (three a day on the free table).
  • A local Council+ flag used only to test the paywall door — not a real subscription.

What we do not collect

This build has no Council user accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no ads, and no crash reporters. We do not get a copy of your questions, answers, or keys.

Live mode and third parties

Demo is the default. Answers are simulated on the device. Council does not call OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google.

If you switch to Live and paste keys, this device calls those APIs directly. Prompts and answers go to the providers you configured. Their policies apply:

  • OpenAI privacy policy
  • Anthropic privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy

Keys stay in localStorage on this device. Council has no server that receives them.

Payments (none yet)

Council+ at $3.99 a month is UI-only. No payments are processed in this build. When in-app purchase exists, Apple or Google will process payment. This page will be updated then.

Children

Council is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Clear the archive in Chamber → This device. That cannot be undone.
  • Delete keys — and everything else Council stored — by clearing this site’s or app’s data.
  • Uninstall the app, or remove the home-screen shortcut.

Changes

If a later build starts collecting more, this page will change. Trust the effective date at the top.
