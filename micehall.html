MiceHall privacy policy

MiceHall is a meeting transcription app from Intelligence vive inc. (Canada).

What we store

MiceHall keeps a local text archive of your meetings: titles, transcripts, markers, and any notes you review (summary, problems, decisions, actions, solutions). You can search, recap, and share that text.

What we do not store

Audio is never stored. Microphone audio is streamed for live transcription and is not saved on the device or on MiceHall servers.

Speech-to-text

Live transcription uses a third-party speech-to-text processor. Audio is sent only to produce the transcript. MiceHall does not keep the audio after that.

Languages

MiceHall supports Persian (fa), English (en), and French (fr).

Accounts

MiceHall requires a login (Google, Facebook, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone). There is no guest mode and no skip. The account is keyed by verified email or Apple user id. The session stays on the device until you sign out. MiceHall does not sell account data.

Sharing

You choose when to copy, share, export, or send a recap to Confluence. Confluence publish uses a server you configure. The app does not contain Atlassian credentials.

Subscriptions

MiceHall Plus is a $15 USD/month auto-renewing subscription sold by Intelligence vive inc.. First 20 minutes of captured meeting time are free. Already-captured meetings stay readable if you do not subscribe.

Feedback

If you send a bug report or an improvement idea, MiceHall opens your mail app and opens a GitHub issue on the MiceHall repo. Bug reports include app version, build, OS, and device. You may attach a screenshot in your mail app. MiceHall does not keep those reports on its servers. The app does not contain a GitHub token.
