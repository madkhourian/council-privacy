SonicShelf Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 October 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Montreal, Quebec, Canada) · App: SonicShelf: Offline Audio (com.donneesfotros.sonicshelf)

SonicShelf: Offline Audio is an Android app from Données Fotros Inc. It plays music, podcasts, and audiobooks already stored on your device, fully offline. There is no account and no server. This policy describes what stays on your device and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • The library index, playback progress, bookmarks, and settings are stored only on the device.
  • App data backup is disabled.

SonicShelf accesses only the folders you pick through Android’s system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). Playback stays on the device.

What we do not collect

  • No account.
  • No server.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

The app has no INTERNET permission. No data is collected.

Permissions

SonicShelf uses a foreground service for media playback only.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your data.
  • We do not share your data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling SonicShelf deletes its data.

Changes
