LocalPilot Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 October 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Montreal, Quebec, Canada) · App: LocalPilot (com.donneesfotros.localpilot)

LocalPilot is an offline phone command assistant for Android from Données Fotros Inc. You type a command such as “flashlight on”, “timer 10 minutes”, or “volume 40%”, and the app runs it on the device. There is no account and no server. This policy describes what stays on your device and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Command history is stored only on the device.
  • Settings are stored only on the device.

Commands run on the device. LocalPilot does not send them to Données Fotros.

What we do not collect

  • No account.
  • No server that receives your commands, history, or settings.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.

The app has no INTERNET permission.

Permissions

LocalPilot may request access only for the commands that need it:

  • Camera flash / torch access, for flashlight commands.
  • Notifications, for timer alerts.
  • Alarm scheduling, for timers.
  • Do Not Disturb access, and the special access to modify system settings, only if you enable those commands.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data.

Data deletion

You can clear command history and settings in the app. Uninstalling LocalPilot removes what the app stored on the device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
