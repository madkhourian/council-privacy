Drop-Pen Privacy Policy

Effective: 28 August 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Canada) · App: Drop-Pen (com.madkhourian.droppen)

Drop-Pen is a student lecture note-taker. This policy describes what the app stores, what leaves your device, and how to contact us.

Consent first

Before recording, Drop-Pen asks for your consent. Do not record other people without their permission. You are responsible for complying with classroom and local recording rules.

What stays on your device

  • Lecture audio you choose to record (local WAV / audio files).
  • Transcripts, study notes, cheat sheets, quizzes, plans, and chat tied to those lectures.
  • App preferences (for example language EN/FR).

Audio is local-first: recordings are saved on your phone so you can keep studying offline. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data can delete that content.

What may leave your device

  • Speech-to-text: when you transcribe, audio or audio-derived data is sent to our transcription provider (AssemblyAI) to produce text. We do not sell your lecture audio.
  • Study tools: when you generate study materials (cheat sheet, roadmap, flashcards, quiz, coach), transcript text is sent to an AI provider (OpenAI) through our backend. The client does not embed API keys.
  • Usage metering: if you use included AI tokens, the backend may track approximate token usage for your account quota. We do not invent fake usage numbers.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal lecture content.
  • We do not use your recordings for advertising profiles.
  • We do not require you to post lectures publicly to use the core note-taking features.

Accounts and billing

If you subscribe later, payment is handled by the store (Google Play / App Store) or a payment processor you connect. Store listings will not invent prices; any student plan terms will be shown in-app and in the store when offered.

Children

Drop-Pen is aimed at students. If you are under the age where parental consent is required in your region, use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Data deletion

Delete a lecture in the app to remove its local files when the app supports that action. You can also clear app storage or uninstall. For backend-associated usage data, email us and we will delete what we control.
