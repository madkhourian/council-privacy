RenderCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Canada) · App: RenderCut (com.donneesfotros.rendercut)

RenderCut is a local-first Android video editor from Données Fotros Inc. The product is simple: edit → render reliably → get your file. This policy describes what the app stores, what stays on your device, and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Media you pick (video and related files) stays on the device. RenderCut does not upload your clips to our servers.
  • Exports are written to storage you choose, using Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) or MediaStore.
  • Background chunked rendering and Safe Mode use a local disk cache and checkpoints only. That cache is cleared when an export finishes, or when you clear the app cache.

RenderCut is offline-first. You can edit and export without an account and without sending your media to us.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in. There is no email/password, Google, Facebook, Apple, or other social login.
  • No ads and no analytics SDK.
  • No crash-reporting or advertising networks in the MVP.

We do not operate a RenderCut backend that receives your videos, project files, or exports.

Permissions

RenderCut requests only what it needs to pick media, write the export you asked for, and show a foreground notification while an export is running. The MVP does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use third-party ad networks.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your media.

Children

RenderCut is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age where parental consent is required in your region, use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Data deletion

Your media remains in the locations you chose. Delete or move those files with the system files or gallery apps. Clear the app cache to remove local render checkpoints. Uninstalling RenderCut removes app-private cache; it does not delete exports you already saved to shared storage.
