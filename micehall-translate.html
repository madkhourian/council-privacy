MiceHall Translate privacy policy

MiceHall Translate is a live meeting transcription and live translation app from Données Fotros Inc. (Canada).

What we store

Meetings store TEXT only: titles, transcripts, translations, and related meeting text. You can search and review that text.

What we do not store

Audio is processed while you record and is never written to disk. Audio and video are never stored. Microphone audio is streamed for live transcription and live translation and is not saved on the device or on MiceHall Translate servers.

Speech-to-text and translation

Live transcription and live translation use a third-party processor. Audio is sent only to produce the transcript and the translation. MiceHall Translate does not keep the audio after that.

Languages

MiceHall Translate supports Persian (fa), English (en), and French (fr). Québec French is French.

Accounts

MiceHall Translate requires a login (email/password, Google, Facebook, or Sign in with Apple). There is no guest mode and no skip. The session stays on the device until you sign out. MiceHall Translate does not sell account data.

Trial

Each account includes 20 minutes of captured meeting time. The trial is counted server-side. Uninstalling the app does not reset the trial. The owner account s.madkhourian@gmail.com is free and unlimited.
