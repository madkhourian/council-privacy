SpecMerge Privacy Policy

Effective: 5 October 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Canada) · App: SpecMerge (com.donneesfotros.specmerge)

SpecMerge is a shopping companion from Données Fotros Inc., published on Google Play as “SpecMerge: Product Compare.” It is not a store. You share product links, selected text, or screenshots from any shopping app, or you enter products yourself. SpecMerge groups duplicate listings into one canonical product, extracts specs, flags conflicting claims, and shows a calm comparison, with no urgency timers and no gamification. Version 1 has no account and no SpecMerge backend. This policy describes what stays on your device and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Products, listings, specs, screenshots, and notes are stored locally in an app-private database on the device.
  • Screenshot text recognition runs on the device with Google ML Kit. SpecMerge does not send screenshot text to us for recognition.
  • If you export, the export stays on the device. SpecMerge does not upload it.

Version 1 does not include an account, sign-in, or a SpecMerge server that receives your products, listings, specs, screenshots, or notes.

Optional link previews

The only network use is optional. When you explicitly share a product link, the device may fetch the public page metadata that site already publishes — the page title, description, and image. That request is sent directly from your device to that site. It is not sent through a SpecMerge server.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of your data.

We do not operate a backend that receives your product library.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state, only so the app can fetch a link preview for a page you explicitly share.
  • Camera, only if you choose to photograph a product or label.
  • The system photo picker for screenshots. SpecMerge does not request broad storage access.
  • Notifications, only if you turn them on.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use third-party ad networks.
  • We do not build advertising or tracking profiles from your products, listings, or screenshots.

Children

SpecMerge is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age where parental consent is required in your region, use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Data deletion

You can delete any product, clear the cache, and use “Delete all data” in Settings. Uninstalling SpecMerge removes all app data. There is no cloud copy for us to delete. Export is local only.
