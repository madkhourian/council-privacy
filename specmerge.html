SpecMerge Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 October 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Canada) · App: SpecMerge (com.donneesfotros.specmerge)

SpecMerge is a shopping companion from Données Fotros Inc., published on Google Play as “SpecMerge: Product Compare.” It is not a store. You share product links, selected text, or screenshots from any shopping app, or you enter products yourself. SpecMerge groups duplicate listings into one canonical product, extracts specs, flags conflicting claims, and shows a calm comparison, with no urgency timers and no gamification. Version 1 has no account and no SpecMerge backend. This policy describes what stays on your device and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Products, listings, specs, screenshots, and notes are stored locally in an app-private database on the device.
  • Screenshot text recognition runs on the device with Google ML Kit. SpecMerge does not send screenshots or recognized text to us. ML Kit still sends diagnostics to Google; see Google ML Kit below.
  • If you export, the export stays on the device. SpecMerge does not upload it.

Version 1 does not include an account, sign-in, or a SpecMerge server that receives your products, listings, specs, screenshots, or notes.

Optional link previews

When you explicitly share a product link, the device may fetch the public page metadata that site already publishes — the page title, description, and image. That request is optional, and it is sent directly from your device to that site. It is not sent through a SpecMerge server. Google ML Kit’s separate network use is described below.

Google ML Kit

Screenshot text recognition uses Google ML Kit and runs on the device. SpecMerge does not send your screenshots or the recognized text to Données Fotros. Google’s ML Kit terms state that this processing happens on the device, and that ML Kit does not send that input or its results to Google servers.

ML Kit does send diagnostics and a per-installation identifier (an install ID) to Google over HTTPS. Google uses that data for diagnostics and usage analytics. The same disclosure lists device information, app information, performance metrics, and related technical events. ML Kit encrypts this data in transit with HTTPS and does not transfer it to other companies. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure for Android.

What we do not collect

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No advertising SDK, and no analytics SDK that we operate. Google ML Kit sends diagnostics and an installation identifier to Google, as described above.
  • No sale of your product library.

We do not operate a backend that receives your product library.

Permissions

SpecMerge requests only these permissions:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET), so the device can fetch an optional link preview for a page you explicitly share, and so Google ML Kit can send the diagnostics described above.
  • Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), so the app can tell whether a network is available.

It does not request camera, notifications, microphone, contacts, location, or broad storage access.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your products, listings, specs, or screenshots with third parties. Google receives the ML Kit diagnostics and installation identifier described above.
  • We do not use third-party ad networks.
  • We do not build advertising or tracking profiles from your products, listings, or screenshots.

Children

SpecMerge is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age where parental consent is required in your region, use the app only with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Data deletion

You can delete any product, clear the cache, and use “Delete all data” in Settings. Uninstalling SpecMerge removes all app data. There is no cloud copy for us to delete. Export is local only.
