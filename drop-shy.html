Drop Your Shyness privacy policy

Drop Your Shyness is a meeting recorder from Données Fotros Inc. (Canada). Bundle ID: com.donneesfotros.dropshy.

What we store

Meetings store TEXT only: titles, transcripts, and related meeting text. Drop Your Shyness keeps a searchable text archive. You can search and review that text.

What we do not store

Raw audio is not the product of record. Drop Your Shyness does not keep meeting audio as the archive. Microphone audio is used for live transcription and is not kept as the product of record on Drop Your Shyness servers.

Speech-to-text

Live transcription uses a third-party processor. Audio is sent only to produce the transcript. Drop Your Shyness does not keep the audio after that as the product of record.

Languages

Drop Your Shyness supports English, Français, and Auto.

Accounts

Drop Your Shyness requires a login (email/password, Google, Facebook when configured, or Sign in with Apple). There is no guest mode and no skip. The session stays on the device until you sign out. Drop Your Shyness does not sell account data.

Trial

Each account includes 20 minutes of captured meeting time. The trial is counted server-side. Uninstalling the app does not reset the trial. The owner account s.madkhourian@gmail.com is free and unlimited.
