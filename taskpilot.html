TaskPilot Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 November 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Montreal, Quebec, Canada) · App: TaskPilot (com.donneesfotros.taskpilot)

TaskPilot is an Android task assistant from Données Fotros Inc. Simple commands run on the device. A complex task can be sent to an AI provider you configure, and only after you review and approve the text that will leave the phone. There is no Données Fotros account and no Données Fotros backend. This policy describes what stays on your device, what can leave it, and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Simple commands are processed on the device. Données Fotros does not receive them.
  • Task history and the offline queue are stored only on the device, and they are encrypted.
  • API keys you enter are encrypted on the device with Android Keystore-backed encryption. Données Fotros does not receive your keys.

There is no Données Fotros backend and no account. The developer does not receive your task contents.

AI providers you configure

For a complex task, TaskPilot may send text directly from your device over HTTPS to an AI provider you configure with your own API key. The request does not pass through a Données Fotros server. It is sent only after you see the outbound text and approve it.

Before that send, TaskPilot attempts to redact personal information on the device. That local redaction is not guaranteed to catch everything. Read the text before you approve it.

The provider you choose receives what you approve. That provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply to the text it receives and to how it handles it. Données Fotros does not control the provider.

Actions that open other apps

Actions such as email, calendar, and sharing are handed off to apps you choose, through Android intents. TaskPilot does not perform those actions itself, and it does not send them through a Données Fotros server. The app you choose handles the action under that app’s own terms and privacy policy.

TaskPilot does not use an AccessibilityService, and it does not automate other apps.

What we do not collect

  • No Données Fotros account.
  • No Données Fotros backend that receives your tasks, history, offline queue, or API keys.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics SDKs.

The developer does not receive your task contents. Text you approve for a complex task goes from your device to the AI provider you configured, as described above.

Permissions

TaskPilot does not request SMS, call log, contacts, location, or camera permissions.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not receive your task contents.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

TaskPilot is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

You can erase all TaskPilot data in Settings > Privacy & Security. Uninstalling the app also removes what it stored on the device. There is no Données Fotros cloud copy for us to delete. Text you already approved and sent to an AI provider is held by that provider under its own policy.
