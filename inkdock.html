InkDock Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 November 2026 · Publisher: Données Fotros Inc. (Montreal, Quebec, Canada) · App: InkDock: Notes & PDF (com.donneesfotros.inkdock)

InkDock: Notes & PDF is an offline-first notebook for Android from Données Fotros Inc. There is no account, no backend, and no Internet permission. This policy describes what stays on your device and how to contact us.

What stays on your device

  • Notes, ink, PDFs, images, and audio recordings are stored only in app-private storage on the device.
  • Android backups are disabled.

There is no account and no Données Fotros backend. The app has no INTERNET permission. Nothing you write, draw, import, or record is sent to Données Fotros.

Microphone

InkDock uses the microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) only when you tap Record. Recording continues only while the editor is visible.

On-device text recognition

OCR runs on the device with bundled ML Kit text recognition. It does not use the network. Recognized text stays on the device.

Import and export

Files are imported through the system file picker or the Photo Picker. Originals are never modified.

Export happens only when you explicitly export a ZIP to a location you choose.

What we do not collect

  • No account.
  • No backend.
  • No Internet permission.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics.
  • No crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking.

No data is collected or shared.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your data.
  • We do not share your data.
  • We do not sell your data.

Children

InkDock is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

You can delete notes or notebooks in the app. You can erase everything in Settings > Erase all data. Uninstalling the app also removes what it stored on the device. There is no Données Fotros cloud copy for us to delete.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
